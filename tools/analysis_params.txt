Parameters of VEGAS's analysis effects from the plug-ins' resource XML (MagixCVFx.xml, MagixAiFx.xml, Stabilize.xml):
type, name, label, choices. Defaults and ranges are not in the XML; they come from the plug-in binaries. Smart Noise Reduction
is in no resource file here, so its two parameters (Noise, EnableEnhancement) are a guess. tools/ofxparams/xmlparams.py rebuilds
this list and compare_defs.py diffs it against analysis_test's DUMPDEFS output.
